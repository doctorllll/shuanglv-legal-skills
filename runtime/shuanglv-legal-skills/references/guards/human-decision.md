# Guard｜Human Decision

`unit_id: unit.guard.human-decision`

**Scope：** Reserved substantive decision guard.

## Trigger
- Plea/waiver/appeal/settlement/major transaction position or equivalent reserved choice must be made.

## Negative Trigger
- Pure analysis that does not cross into making the reserved decision for the human.

## Essential Procedure
1. Identify reserved decision.
2. Provide analysis/options.
3. Require human choice; do not silently decide.

## Deepening Conditions
- Irreversible/high-consequence choice.

## Exit Sufficiency
- Human decision is obtained or work stops before acting as if decided.

## Professional Results
- decision options + explicit human confirmation state


## Reserved Human Decision Contract

- System may analyze/recommend but must not silently make the reserved decision.
- A human decision does not itself authorize external execution of that decision.

Examples: plea, waiver, appeal, settlement, major contract/transaction position, equivalent substantive choice
