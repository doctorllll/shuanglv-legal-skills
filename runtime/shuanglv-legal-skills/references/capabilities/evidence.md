# Evidence｜证据与证明分析

`unit_id: unit.cap.evidence`

**Scope：** Generic fact-to-evidence and proof-sufficiency analysis.

## Trigger
- Directive asks whether a proposition is proved/disproved or what evidence supports it.
- Legal conclusion turns on reliability, contradiction, legality, independence or missing proof.
- Domain extension requests a proof analysis delta.

## Negative Trigger
- Pure legal drafting/rewrite.
- Pure legal research not dependent on proof.
- FORMAT_ONLY task.

## Essential Procedure
1. Identify material proposition to prove.
2. Map supporting/opposing evidence and source state.
3. Assess relevance, reliability, legality, independence and contradictions.
   - **Common-source rule：** multiple documents, statements or records derived from the same underlying source do not become multiple independent corroborating sources merely because they appear in separate files/channels. Trace provenance before counting independence.
4. Identify missing proof and burden/sufficiency consequence.

## Deepening Conditions
- High-stakes proof dispute.
- Conflicting evidence sources.
- Criminal/civil domain adds proof-standard or exclusion delta.

## Exit Sufficiency
- Material propositions have an evidence position and residual uncertainty is explicit.

## Professional Results
- fact→evidence map
- evidence conflicts
- reliability/legality/independence findings
- missing-evidence list
- proof sufficiency result
